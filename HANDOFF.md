# Handoff

The editorial revision is paused at a reproducible draft. The private transfer ZIP contains the draft, source material, editing files, verification output and a full restart guide. It is intentionally not tracked on this public branch.

The editorial decisions are to distinguish shortcomings in an assessment from shortages of opportunities, separate personal accounts from evidence of wider effects, and leave gaps in first-person reporting for the author instead of supplying details. The structure is assembled; the remaining work is to resolve those gaps, verify the supporting claims and references, and make the separate prose pass before delivering the complete manuscript.

See [PROJECT_HANDOFF.md](PROJECT_HANDOFF.md) for the checkpoint and recovery steps.
